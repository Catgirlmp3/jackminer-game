# JackMiner ♠️

**The house is waiting. Think you can beat it?**

JackMiner is a strange little blackjack game with a casino-sized attitude and a rather unusual buy-in: your PC's spare processing power.

You walk in with **€1,000 in fake money**, a pocketful of chips and absolutely no guarantee you'll walk out richer. The table is locked until you choose to start a resource session. Then the cards are yours, the house is watching, and your luck is officially on the clock.

## Your seat is waiting

- **Play your way:** hit, stand, double down or split. The dealer stands on soft 17.
- **Make it count:** blackjack pays 3:2. Opening bets go up to €300.
- **Choose your pressure:** Easy, Medium, Hard… or **O KURWA**. Pick your poison.
- **Collect questionable treasures:** mayo, suspicious pickles, a 2003 Corolla and other items nobody asked for.
- **Check back for House Drops:** a little bonus becomes claimable every 15 minutes while the game is open.
- **Keep your progress:** your virtual bankroll and collection are saved between sessions.

## A tiny word from the house

The bankroll is fictional. The workload is not.

JackMiner uses your CPU during a resource session. That means real heat, fan noise, power use and electricity costs. Choose a mode that your cooling and your ears can tolerate. Nothing starts without your confirmation, and stopping the session locks the blackjack table again.

There is **no real-money gambling**: no deposits, no withdrawals and no cash value. Just you, the cards and a house with questionable vibes.
